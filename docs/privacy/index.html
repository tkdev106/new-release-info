Privacy Policy

New Releases (Android app)
Last updated: November 23, 2026

日本語

This policy explains how the developer of New Releases ("we") handles information in the app. The app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not sell any information.

1. Information kept on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Artists you follow, and the releases found for them
  • Play counts, if you turn on notification access
  • Your settings

Notification access is used only to read what is playing in your music apps (the artist name and the app), to show your most-played artists. The app does not read the content of your notifications. This is optional, and you can turn it off at any time in your device settings.

If Android backup is on, this information may be backed up to your Google account by Android.

2. Information sent off your device

Sent toWhatWhy
AppleSearch terms and the IDs of artists you followTo find artists and their new and upcoming releases
Our server (hosted on Cloudflare)The names of artists you followTo get artist suggestions, only when you tap the refresh button
Google (Gemini API)Our server passes on only those artist namesTo generate the suggestions
Google (Play Integrity API)Information generated by Google Play servicesTo confirm that requests come from the genuine app

Google may use the artist names sent to the Gemini API to improve its products and machine learning technologies, and they may be read by human reviewers. Nothing that identifies you is sent with them.

Our server keeps suggestion results for up to 7 days so it can answer the same request again. They are not linked to you.

Each recipient handles information under its own privacy policy: Apple, Cloudflare, Google.

3. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone other than the recipients above, except where required by law.

4. Your choices

  • Turn off notification access to stop counting plays.
  • Don't tap the refresh button for suggestions if you don't want artist names sent to our server.
  • Uninstall the app, or clear its storage, to delete all information on your device.

5. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add features. We will post the new version on this page with its date.

6. Contact

Please contact us at the developer email address on the app's Google Play page, or through GitHub Issues.
