Privacy Policy

New Releases (Android app)
Effective date and last updated: November 23, 2026

日本語

This Privacy Policy explains how tkdev, an individual developer ("we", "us"), handles information in the Android app "New Releases" (the "App"). Please read it before using the App. If the English and Japanese versions differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Our approach

  • The App has no accounts. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.
  • Information the App handles is, in principle, stored only on your device and never reaches us.
  • When the App must send information off the device to provide a feature, it sends only what is necessary, and this policy states what is sent and to whom.
  • The App shows no ads, uses no analytics, and we do not sell any information.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored in the App's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else (except as described in Section 3).

InformationWhat and why
Artists you addNames, IDs and similar details of artists you add. Used to check for new releases.
Release informationTitles, release dates, artwork URLs and similar details of releases found. Used for the list, notifications and the widget.
Play countsOnly if you turn on notification access: the artist name of the song playing in your music apps, which app played it, and how many times and when. Used to show your most-played artists and to choose which music app opens a release.
Settings and app stateWidget style, hidden items, last check time and other information the App needs to work.

Notification access

The App uses Android's notification access only to read the "now playing" information that music apps publish (media sessions). It does not read the content of your notifications, such as messages or emails. This permission is optional; before you turn it on, the App shows an explanation and asks for your consent. You can turn it off at any time in your device settings.

Device backup

If Android's automatic backup is on, Android may back up the information above to your own Google account. That backup is managed by Google and we cannot access it.

4. Handling and retention on our relay server

  • To answer the same combination of artists quickly, we cache a value derived from the combination of artist names, together with the suggestions, for up to 7 days. It is not linked to anything that identifies you.
  • We use IP addresses temporarily to limit rapid repeated requests and prevent abuse. Request logs are kept for a short period by Cloudflare's systems for troubleshooting.
  • We count the total number of uses per day, but only the count; we do not record who used it.
  • We do not use this information to identify individual users.

5. How we use information

We use information only to:

  • Provide the App's features (release checks, notifications, the widget, most-played artists and suggestions)
  • Prevent abuse and keep the service running reliably
  • Fix problems and respond to inquiries

6. Sharing with third parties

Except for the transmissions in Section 3, we do not share your information with third parties, unless required or permitted by law.

7. Your choices

  • Stop counting plays: turn off notification access for the App in your device settings.
  • Don't use suggestions: if you never tap the refresh button for suggestions, nothing is sent to our relay server.
  • Stop notifications: turn off the App's notifications in your device settings.
  • Delete your data: clear the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App, to delete all information on the device.

8. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS). Secrets such as the relay server's keys are not included in the App and are kept on the server side.

9. Age

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

10. Availability

The App is not offered in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom or Switzerland. Suggestions are not available from those regions.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as we add features or as laws change. If the information collected or its recipients change, we will show the changes and the update date on this page, and announce important changes in the App's release notes or similar.

12. Contact

For questions about this policy or how the App handles information, contact us at the developer email address shown on the App's Google Play page, or via GitHub Issues.
